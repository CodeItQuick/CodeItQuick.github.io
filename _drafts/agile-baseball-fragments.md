# Every few years, baseball discovers the correct way to play baseball

The imitators copied what Oakland bought. They believed they were copying how Oakland thought.

Moneyball was never "walks are undervalued." It was "find what the market has mispriced and buy it cheap." The moment everyone valued on-base percentage, a faithful Moneyball team would have abandoned on-base percentage. Almost nobody did. They kept buying walks and called it analytics.

---

First it is a useful answer to one problem. Then it becomes a philosophy. Finally, its advocates explain all of baseball through it.

---

The defensive shift worked. It kept working. By 2022 teams were shifting on 38% of plate appearances, and in 2023 MLB changed the rules to stop them.

A tactic can be correct, spread on merit, and still end with the league legislating against it — not because it failed, but because it succeeded everywhere at once and made the game worse to watch.

---

Players cannot trade for a shortstop during a postgame meeting.

They can change how they call for a fly ball. They can change the game plan for the next series with the coaches. They cannot redesign the farm system — and a postgame meeting that ends with "we'll communicate better" when the actual problem is the farm system has not identified an action, it has laundered a structural failure into a personal one.

---

Every few years, baseball discovers the correct way to play baseball. One group of coaches insists the game is won through fundamentals, another through statistics, another through carefully scripted matchups, and another by trusting experienced players to read what happens on the field. Each school can point to a championship team that seemed to prove it right, and each can point to a last-place team that supposedly proves the others wrong. When their own system fails, the players lacked discipline, management interfered, or the coaches did not follow it closely enough. When a rival succeeds, it was luck, exceptional talent, or secretly an example of their philosophy all along.
